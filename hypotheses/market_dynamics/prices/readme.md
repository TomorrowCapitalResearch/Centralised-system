# Price levels, revisits and reversion

[Category index](../START_HERE.md) · [Source guide](../source_index/Sources.md)

**Which levels draw price back, and what happens at a touch?** Volume profiles, point of control, VWAP, moving anchors and barrier outcomes.

In BTC the older high-volume-zone revisit rate was 28.4% against 27.1% after matching. Yesterday’s POC had lifts of 0.93–1.08 across the prior comparisons. Much of the apparent attraction disappears when the target is made equally reachable. (LOG/V-08, LOG/V-12, PREV.)

**Test design.** Freeze the reference price and band at signal time unless the hypothesis explicitly calls for a moving reference. Start the clock on a confirmed exit. Include no-touch cases, match initial distance and count a revisit separately from a bounce.
