# Jumps, precursors and recovery

[Category index](../START_HERE.md) · [Source guide](../source_index/Sources.md)

**Will a shock arrive, repeat, reverse or extend?** First events, aftershocks, polarity, waiting times and post-event recovery.

T-08 concerns sparse major events with gaps measured in days. In a different one-minute study, two or more recent shocks in five minutes preceded a main shock within the hour 27.9% of the time vs 8.5% otherwise. Its matched difference was +15.7 percentage points. These results can coexist. (T-08, LOG/SE-03, HYP.)

**Test design.** Store event size, sign, threshold source, clock, declustering rule and period at risk. Distinguish first events after quiet periods from aftershocks. Use a common event definition when comparing studies.
